About MCQs Tayyari

MCQs Tayyari is a free test-preparation platform built for students and job aspirants preparing for PPSC (Punjab Public Service Commission) examinations in Pakistan.

Our mission is simple: make quality exam preparation accessible to everyone. We organize subject-wise multiple-choice questions aligned with the PPSC one-paper pattern — 100 MCQs, 90 minutes, negative marking — so aspirants can practice exactly what the exam demands.

  • Subject-wise MCQ practice with explanations
  • A true exam simulator mirroring the official pattern
  • Solved past papers, structured as timed mocks
  • Basic Mathematics — a subject most competitors skip
  • 100% free — no login or subscription required

MCQs Tayyari is an independent educational platform and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or officially connected to PPSC or any government body.

Data Sources & Attribution

Our question bank is built only from openly-licensed, public-domain, and official sources. We never copy content from commercial MCQ websites. Licensed datasets are used under their respective licenses with attribution:

  • UrduMMLU — native Urdu exam MCQs, used under CC-BY-4.0. Credit: MBZUAI — Tabassum et al., huggingface.co/datasets/MBZUAI/UrduMMLU.
  • MedMCQA — medical entrance MCQs, used under Apache-2.0.
  • AQuA — algebraic word-problem MCQs, used under Apache-2.0.
  • MMLU — multi-task MCQs, used under the MIT License.
  • AI2 ARC (Allen Institute for AI) — science reasoning MCQs, used under CC BY-SA 4.0, huggingface.co/datasets/allenai/ai2_arc.
  • Official sample papers — NTS and CTSP sample papers published publicly by the testing services themselves, used as published.
  • Islamic Quiz (Muslim Council of Hong Kong) — Islamiyat MCQs, used with the publisher's explicit permission for educational use, muslimcouncil.org.hk.

  • AGIEval (Microsoft) — English and reasoning MCQs (SAT / LogiQA / Gaokao-English), used under the MIT License.
