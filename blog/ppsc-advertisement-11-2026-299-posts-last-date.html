PPSC Advertisement 11/2026: 299 Posts — Last Date 26 October 2026

📅 October 25, 2026✍️ MCQs Tayyari Team⏱️ 9 min read

The Punjab Public Service Commission announced Advertisement No. 11/2026 on 9 October 2026, opening 299 posts across 13 recruitment cases in seven Punjab departments — from Senior Registrars in teaching hospitals to Intelligence Officers in the Punjab Police Special Branch. Online applications close on 26 October 2026, which means you have only a few days left.

This guide covers every post in the advertisement, the eligibility rules, the exact application steps (including the PSID fee payment that trips up first-time applicants), and how to prepare for the one-paper MCQ test that follows.

A note on sources: the details below are compiled from published summaries of the official advertisement (Ref. SPL-999, Daily Dunya, 9 October 2026). Always confirm the exact requirements on the official PPSC website before you apply or pay any fee.

🔑 Key Takeaways

  • Ad No. 11/2026: 299 posts, 13 cases, 7 departments — announced 9 Oct 2026.
  • Last date: 26 October 2026 — apply online only at ppsc.gop.pk.
  • All posts are on contract (2–5 years); Punjab domicile required for every case.
  • Fee is paid via PSID (JazzCash, Easypaisa, ATM, 1Link) — paying the fee is not the same as submitting the form.
  • Selection starts with a 100-MCQ one-paper test — start timed practice now.

Eligibility: The Non-Negotiables

  • Punjab domicile is required for every case — candidates from other provinces cannot apply.
  • Qualification must match your case: DVM with valid PVMC registration for the livestock posts; MBBS with FCPS/MS/MD (or recognised equivalent) plus registration for Senior Registrar; relevant engineering or accounts qualifications for the PHATA posts. Read your case's column carefully — applying with the wrong degree is the most common rejection.
  • Contract, not permanent: every post in this advertisement is on contract of 2 to 5 years (several extendable on performance).
  • Government employees must apply through their department and produce an NOC where required.
  • Foreign or equivalent qualifications need an equivalence certificate ready.

How to Apply Online (Before 26 October)

  1. Open the official PPSC website and read the full Advertisement No. 11/2026 for your post — not a summary, the actual ad.
  2. Match your qualification, age, domicile and experience against the requirements as they stand on the closing date.
  3. Create or log in to your PPSC online account and fill the application form for your post's case number.
  4. Pay the fee via PSID — through an ATM, mobile/internet banking, a 1Link member bank branch, JazzCash, Easypaisa or UPaisa.
  5. Submit the form before 26 October 2026 and save a copy of your application and the fee receipt.

The mistake that kills the most applications: paying the fee is not submitting the form. The PSID payment is step one of a two-step process — your application only counts once the online form itself is submitted. And don't leave it for the last evening: the portal slows down near every deadline.

How to Prepare for the Test

PPSC announces the test schedule and syllabus separately on its website, but the standard pattern for these one-paper recruitments is the familiar one: 100 MCQs, 100 marks, 90 minutes, with −0.25 negative marking — roughly 80% post-related questions and 20% general ability. With the deadline this close, preparation has to be sharp, not broad:

  • Post-related portion first: for Intelligence Officer, that means general knowledge, current affairs, Pak Studies and Islamiat; for Accounts Officer, accounting and commerce fundamentals; for Sports Officer, sports GK plus general subjects. Revise the subjects your post actually tests.
  • Train under the clock: 90 minutes for 100 MCQs is 54 seconds per question. Run full 100-MCQ practice sets with the timer on, so exam-day pacing feels automatic.
  • Respect the −0.25: blind guessing on this paper is a losing strategy — four wrong answers erase one correct one. Read our negative marking strategy guide before your first mock.
  • Use recalled papers honestly: PPSC doesn't publish official past papers, so work from memory-based papers and our past papers section — never from "leaked paper" claims.

How many posts are in PPSC Advertisement 11/2026?

299 posts across 13 recruitment cases — including 107 Senior Registrars, 101 livestock posts, 37 Tehsil Sports Officers, 30 Intelligence Officers, 12 Assistant Accounts Officers, 8 Assistant Regional Managers and 2 Deputy Directors (Engineering).

Are these permanent government jobs?

No. Every post in this advertisement is on contract, ranging from 2 to 5 years depending on the post, with several extendable on performance.

No. Every case in Advertisement 11/2026 requires a Punjab domicile.
