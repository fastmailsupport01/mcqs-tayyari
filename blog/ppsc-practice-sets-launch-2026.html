How to use the 20 sets (a simple plan)

  1. Weeks 1–2: learn the clock. Attempt one set every two days in a single 90-minute sitting. Don't pause the timer. Your first scores don't matter — your pace does.
  2. After every set: study your mistakes. Open the score summary and re-solve every wrong answer. Write the correct fact in an error notebook — this notebook becomes your revision material.
