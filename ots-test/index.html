📚 How to Prepare for OTS

Smart OTS preparation is about pattern, practice and revision — not just reading more books. Here is a practical plan built around this site’s free tools:

  1. Master the pattern first. Study the exam pattern table above — questions, time limit, marks and negative marking — before attempting a single MCQ.
  2. Build subject-wise depth. Practice one subject at a time with explanations on, so every mistake teaches you something new.
  3. Train under time pressure. Take the timed OTS mock test regularly — answering against the clock builds the speed and accuracy the real test demands.
  4. Revise your weak areas. Re-attempt the questions you got wrong and drill the subjects where your score is lowest.
  5. Stay current. Current affairs and everyday science change every month — keep practicing fresh MCQs right up to exam week.
